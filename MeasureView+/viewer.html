About

Microscope MeasureView. A browser-based tool for viewing, calibrating, and measuring microscope images.

Hotkeys
Usage

- Modes: Use hotkeys (like M for Measure) or the context menu (right-click) to change modes. ESC key whilst in those modes cancels them. C-A-M-Del will delete the in progress measurment in those modes.

- Calibration: Press C, Use a ruler in an image, click two points and enter the known distance. Use ~50% of img width for good cal distance. A saved calibration can be reused for images that use the same object lens and are at the same distance from the lens even if the pixel resolution/aspect ratio is changed.

- In View Mode: (Esc) clears all after confirmation, click near any endpoint to move it. Hover near a line to highlight and grab it's label.
